## Citation and limitations

- Cite `[Sxx]` next to scientific statements and identify the actual dataset/product used.
- Record original acquisition/access dates; do not manufacture a new verification date during translation.
- Label project calculations and point to their script.
- State whether evidence is an observation, model, interpretation or assumption.
- The review was exploratory, with incomplete author/metadata fields in some records and no completed external expert audit.
- Some sources were consulted through abstracts or open versions; not every full paper was available.
- Data and links can change. Verify the original record and product-specific terms before reuse.
- Generative AI is disclosed in [25](25_Workflows_Tools_AI_and_Impact.md), not cited as scientific evidence.

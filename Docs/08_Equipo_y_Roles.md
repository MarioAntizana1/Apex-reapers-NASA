## Responsibility rules

- One accountable owner per deliverable; collaborators do not imply unlimited workload.
- Mario coordinates integration; scientific claims need Alessandro's review or a recorded qualified substitute.
- Everyone tests their own contribution; QA is not a substitute for author responsibility.
- First-time contributors receive small tasks, examples, pairing and review.
- Communicate a blocker promptly; do not silently hold a critical dependency.

## Collaboration workflow

1. Agree on outcome and acceptance criterion.
2. Agree on coordinate, height, route and metadata contracts before independent production.
3. Integrate a small sample early.
4. Use Git/GitHub for reviewable code/document changes; share raw large working files separately.
5. Preserve others' uncommitted work and record integration decisions.
6. Update source cards and tests alongside the affected feature.

Use short status updates: completed, next and blocked. Protect breaks/rest and maintain a backup owner for critical integration and communication work.

## Onboarding

| Area | First bounded task |
|---|---|
| Science | Review one claim and prepare a cited data/POI card |
| Design | Walk through one user flow and record a usability issue |
| Data | Inspect one product's CRS, units, coverage and rights |
| QA | Reproduce one failure and write expected versus actual behavior |
| Visual assets | Supply one lightweight asset with provenance, scale and orientation |
| 3D | Load one checked terrain sample and prove a recoverable exit to 2D |

## Charter

Make a clear, useful and honest demonstration. Cite evidence, expose uncertainty, communicate blockers, credit contributors and prefer a working core to unsupported promises.

The research log retains historical roster decisions; the public roster should be confirmed by the team before official submission.
